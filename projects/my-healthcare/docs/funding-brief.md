# My Healthcare — Founder / Funding Brief

## One-line description

My Healthcare is an early-stage concept for a daily health companion that helps Indian users make practical food and nutrition decisions using their preferences, routine, goals, and budget.

## Problem

Everyday nutrition decisions can be confusing and inconvenient. Generic diet plans may not reflect a person's real food preferences, local meals, schedule, or budget. This problem statement must be validated through customer interviews.

## Proposed solution

A simple experience combining:
- food preference setup;
- daily meal logging;
- transparent nutrition estimates;
- editable, budget-aware meal suggestions;
- weekly summaries;
- manual tracking with optional, consent-based health-platform integrations.

## Initial wedge

Start with one user segment and one repeated daily decision. Do not launch as an all-purpose medical platform. Select the segment only after user interviews.

## Why now / differentiation hypothesis

The product hypothesis is that a more practical, preference-aware daily decision flow can be more useful to some users than a tracker focused primarily on past activity. This is a hypothesis, not a proven competitive advantage. Test it against existing nutrition and health apps.

## Current stage

Idea / product specification. A functioning implementation, users, traction, clinical validation, and integrations are **not confirmed by this document**. Update this section only with verifiable evidence.

## Near-term milestones

1. Interview 10–20 target users.
2. Choose a narrow initial segment.
3. Build a small demonstrable prototype.
4. Test the core onboarding-to-suggestion flow.
5. Record feedback, limitations, and iteration.
6. Prepare a short demo and evidence-backed pitch deck.
7. Apply to suitable early-stage programs, including healthcare-focused incubators where eligibility fits.

## Funding use hypothesis

Potential uses, subject to the program's rules:
- prototype engineering and testing;
- nutrition data quality and evaluation;
- privacy/security review;
- user research and pilot operations;
- responsible product design and localization.

## Founder pitch draft

"I'm building My Healthcare, a daily health companion focused on a practical question: what should I eat now, given my food preferences, routine, goals, and budget? I'm starting with a narrow nutrition workflow, validating it with users, and building toward transparent, personalized guidance rather than another dashboard of numbers. The current stage is early concept/prototype development, and my next milestone is to validate the problem and demonstrate the core user journey."
